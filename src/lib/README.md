# src/lib

Plain TypeScript shared by the app, the lab and the node harnesses. No components.

## Start here

- `platform.ts`: `detectTier`, `GpuClass`, `QualityRung`.
- `lookFade.ts`: `lookOf`, `lookStep`: changing look without a cut.
- `sceneMap.ts`: the patch bay, `PATCH_TARGETS`, `cordReading`.
- `audioFeatures.ts`: named bands and onsets, `SOUND_SOURCES`; it re-exports `analyserEmulator`.
- `pulseEar.ts`: the beat whatever plays it (`PulseEar`), owned by the ear: the kick where a kick keeps time, the band's whole-range attack (SuperFlux) where none does, entrained to the band's period so a melody note between beats is not the beat; it is the reading's `beat`. `hitSource.ts`'s `beatAt(reading)` is the time of the last beat, `beat ?? onsets.kick`, for the beat clock and Kick hit.
- `checkHooks.ts`: every `window.chromaglass*` and `window.__cg*` name the app and the checks share.

## Contracts

- Never imports `components/`, `app/`, `sim/`. From `hooks/` only the type `AudioData` (`sceneMap`, `songTrack`); from `gpu/` only `MAGNET_RADIUS` (`magnetSize`). Otherwise `src/types` and `src/constants`.
- `src/gpu` imports a handful from here (dye, turntable, slosh, rimMask, deltaRows, crashLog and others), and its `wgsl/` only number-only ones (turntable, dye, liquidProps, handSolid, filmTable); `sim/` and `hooks/` import many.
- `useSignedIn`, `cloudSyncApp` and `accountShows` export React hooks; `songMapWorker` and `fingerprintIndexWorker` are Web Worker entries.
- `licenseKey` and `cloudSync` are the device's half of `server/license-key.js` and `server/cloud-sync.js`; `remoteProtocol` is the wire `server/remote-server.js` relays (its `assistant` role and messages since PLAN 44b; its `tv` role, `tv-join`, `tv-leave`, `tv-show`, `tv-signal` and `TvSignal` since PLAN 12-tv). `liveShow` exports `startSavedLive(test)`, the panel's Go to the saved destinations, for the assistant's Full mode. `padPicture` exports `wantStill`, `padStillWidth`, `PAD_STILL_MAX_WIDTH`, `PAD_STILL_MAX_CHARS` and `PAD_STILL_WAIT_MS` (the assistant's one 1280 still, PLAN 44c-4); `songShows` exports the `pour` action, `parseSongAction`, `pourGesture` and `SONG_SHOW_TICK_MS` (PLAN 44f). `pourText` exports `parsePourText` (a `pour-text` message read as untrusted), `pourTextRows` (typed words laid out as a title card) and the `POUR_TEXT_*` caps (PLAN 37x-10c); `remoteProtocol` carries the message.

## Files

About 160 files, grouped; a name is its file.

**Fluid physics.** beads: oil beads. breath: Blow as air. bubbleDye: dye round a bubble. bubbleLiquid: the liquid under a bubble from the species field (`liquidAtCell`, `pouredOf`, PLAN 33f). cover: the liquid under the cover glass and the gap it holds it at (`coverStep`, `stefanSettle`, PLAN 38r-5). bubbles: trapped air as pockets in the gap (BubbleField: `liquid(settings)`, `species(snap, poured)`, `step(dt, velocity, tiltX, tiltY, agitation)`, `pack()`). chemistry: live reactions. deltaRows: CPU rows to hand the GPU. detailFlow: closeup cell clock. dripPace: drop fall time. dye: absorbance. dyeTarget: fill target, and the pours' room under it (a hit's, hitRoomOf, goes past it). filmTable: thin-film colour. handCarry: hand carries ferro. handSolid: Finger as solid. lampBoil: lamp heat. liquidColour: pour colour. liquidFile: liquid files. liquidPhase: which liquid where. liquidProps: species. magnetSize: magnet reach. oilLay: Clock Glass oil. phIndicator: pH from the acid and base poured (and its WGSL twin). phasePour: ferro pour. plateRock: tipping the dish (`rockPast(R)` and `ROCK_PAST`: Plate Rock at the real gap's pace up to the default, the 6 mm gap's at full, PLAN 38r-9; `rockStep` hands the solver its rock). pressRing: press squeeze. rimMask: cells inside the rim. roomStir: room to liquid. sheet: rhythm sheet. slosh: two-layer slosh. soapFilm: soap film reference. squish: top glass. stir: turbulence. turingInk: Turing colour. turntable: spinning dish, and the glass gap (`DISH_GAP_METRES`, 2 mm, the one number; `DISH_REST_GAP` the field's 0.03 in gap units; `DISH_GAP_FIELD_METRES` and `DISH_GAP_FIELD_UV` turn a field value into a length, PLAN 38r). videoFlow: a video as dye and flow. inkTitle: a song's title as ink and the wash that sweeps it (`inkTitleCue`, `inkTitleMask`, `inkTitleWash`, PLAN 37x-10); inkTitleRun: its frame-loop side (`queueInkTitle`, `stepInkTitle`).

**Rendering, projector, recording.** ambient: TV mode. moire: the moiré pack's sheet designs (`MOIRE_SHEETS`). stageClocks: the gel wheel's and the moiré sheet's own clocks. backLook: back plate. drawGate: draw clock. flashGuard: no strobe. governor: quality rung. intro: opening mark. lampKelvin: lamp warmth. frameTaps: frame task's canvas copies. tvFeed: the plate to a TV at home as WebRTC video (`TvFeedSender`, `tapTvFeed`, `preferTvCodecs`, `withStartBitrate`, PLAN 12-tv). liveFrame: stream's frames from the frame task. liveStream: live stream encoder. macroCamera: closeup. markFit: logo for cast. muxMp4, muxShared, muxWebm: muxers. outputConfig: projector and wall. plateSources: surface sources. recordChoice: Record button. render: song render. showClock: live or fixed clock. stepClock: steps a frame. stillWait: how long the opening waits for the page's frames before its first compile (`npm run stillwait`).

**Sound.** analyserEmulator: offline analyser. audioCalibration: room trim. audioFeatures: bands, onsets. barGrid: strong beats (`tell` takes Link's bar). beatClock: beats ahead. chroma: note classes. earClock: hidden-window ear. evolution: per-track seed. fft: FFT. fingerprint: song ID proxy. fingerprintIndexBuild, fingerprintIndexWorker: local index. followedPlace: place in song. handSounds: operator's clicks. hitSource: hits for patches. pulseEar: the beat without a kick drum. localFingerprint: local ID. lyrics: LRCLIB. melodyWalk: the melody across the plate. musicDb: IndexedDB. musicLibrary: shipped music. musicTypes: types. phrasing: breaths. pitchFine: melody pitch. plateDrone: plate as band. simulatedMusic: fake band. songBoundary: song ends. songFollow: live follow. songMap, songMapAnalysis, songMapWorker: song maps. songRef: song match. songScore: timeline. songShape: builds, drops. songTrack: render's ear. soundLevels: levels. systemTap: Mac System audio. tempo: tempo sources. tempoPace: speed to tempo.

**Show.** areaCords: cords per area. assistantLook: a look the owner's assistant armed (PLAN 44c), room keys stripped. autoMap: learn a controller. cameras: camera choice. cartoons: shipped cartoons. controllerSurface: controller faces. drift: Evolve wander. lookFade: look change. lucky: backdrop colours. midi: bindings (`curveOf`: a dial's travel curve, from `LEARNABLE_SETTINGS` or `PANEL_CURVES`; `liveBindings(map, source, bank)` and `pressedBy(event)` for Program Change and bank resolution, PLAN 14t-pc). midiTouch: touch flash. mixer: picture stack. mixFade: row fades. modulators: shapes. padColours: pad palette. performanceTake: takes. plateAreas: dish areas, and the square dish a look with them is laid on (layAngle). ride: fader ride. rng: show seed. sanitizeSettings: patch clamp. sceneMap: patch bay. scenePacing: pacing. sceneSense: room camera. sequencer: show sequence. setList: set list. songDesign: Listen, the song named and its show designed from its map (`designFromMap`, `listenStep`, `ListenControl`; PLAN 28h-8). showKit: show export. slowEvolve: Evolve timing. songShows: per-song show. soundLearn: sound triggers. timecode: MIDI timecode. userPresets: preset files. videoSense: video reading.

**Desk and phone helpers.** controlHelp: hover text. deskLayout: layouts. deskPins: pinnable faders (`PIN_RANGE`; a widened dial's `wanderSpan` is the old width Evolve still wanders by). deskTabs: tabs, and `mergeStoredTabs`, the first meeting of two devices' tab lists (cloud sync, PLAN 8-tabs-a). knobDrag: knob drag. phone: phone layout, tilt. playGesture: Play's fingers. qr: QR code. readout: value text. settingsMap: settings map. slideTray, strobeRing, tapeReels, vuMeter: desk dials. tipDelay, titleTips: tips. tipPad: the Tip pad's arithmetic (a drag as Gravity and Tilt Direction, `padTilt`, `tipGrab`, `tipLetGo`). toolAmount: per-tool amount.

**Accounts and licence.** accountLicense: account key. accountShows: shows directory. authFlag: signed-in cache. cloudSync, cloudSyncApp: Pro sync. firebaseAuth: sign-in (Google, Apple, email; `isApplePrivateRelay`). licenseKey: key check. macSeat: Mac seats. tierGates: tier gates (and `canUseMoirePack`, `hasLicensePack`, `isPackLocked`, the art packs; `getActiveLicenseInfo` returns the key's `packs`). checkoutLinks: the store's buy links (`CHECKOUT_MOIRE_URL` for the pack). licenseKey also exports `normalizePacks` and `sameIdKeyWins` (which re-signed key replaces the held one). useSignedIn: accounts on.

**Mac and iPhone bridges.** appLink: iPhone app link. castProtocol: casting. macApp: Mac download. oscOut: OSC out's switch, its target rule and what is remembered (PLAN 13.5-osc; the server's `osc.js` sends). padPicture: remote's picture. remoteProtocol: phone remote.

**App infrastructure.** appVersion: `APP_VERSION`, the release's name. bench: grid sweep. checkHooks: the checks' `?debug` doors. crashLog: black box. feedback: Settings → Feedback's note and its send (PLAN 39a); feedbackScrub: what a note must not carry. platform: where it runs. showPath: site paths. unhandled: exhaustive switch.
