# Licence history

ChromaGlass has not always been under the licence it is under now. This file
says exactly what applied to what, because "it used to be MIT" is the kind of
thing that turns into a dispute years later and costs nothing to write down now.

| Versions | Licence |
|---|---|
| **1.2.0 and earlier** | MIT |
| **1.3.0 onward** | [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE), converting to Apache 2.0 on 2030-09-19 |

## What that means in practice

**The MIT grant on 1.2.0 and earlier is permanent.** A licence already given
cannot be taken back. Anyone who obtained a copy of ChromaGlass at or before
1.2.0 keeps MIT rights to *that code*, for ever, including the right to fork it
and sell what they build. The change applies to work published from 1.3.0
onward and to nothing before it.

**Using it and performing with it is free.** The Additional Use Grant is
deliberately wide: run it, modify it, play a show with it, charge for that show,
sell the video it renders. None of that needs permission or payment. The
visuals a plate produces are yours.

**Selling it is not.** What the licence reserves is offering ChromaGlass itself
— or something derived from it — to other people as a product: as software, as a
hosted service, or on a box. That is the only thing it stops, and it stops it
because that is the thing the author may want to do.

**It opens on its own.** On 2030-09-19 version 1.3.0 becomes Apache 2.0
automatically, with no action required from anybody, and every later version
opens four years after it is first published. The restriction has an end date
written into it from the start.

## Why this licence

MIT was the right default for a project nobody had seen and the wrong one for a
project someone might sell. The alternative usually reached for —
PolyForm Noncommercial — would have banned the intended users, because a VJ
running a light show at a paid gig is commercial use. The line worth drawing is
not commercial against non-commercial. It is *performing with it* against
*selling it*, and BSL is the licence that draws that line.
