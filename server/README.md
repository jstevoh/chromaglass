# server

What runs off the page: the local show server the laptop and phones meet at, and the Cloudflare Workers for licences, crash reports and song ID. Plain JavaScript modules.

## Start here

- `remote-server.js` (40 KB, grep it): the show server (`npm run remote`): serves the built app on the LAN, relays the remote's messages between laptop and phones over WebSocket (and a TV's WebRTC handshake with the laptop, the `tv` role, PLAN 12-tv), takes OSC, Art-Net and Ableton Link, sends OSC out. Exports `setOsc`/`oscState` and `setOscOut`/`oscOutState` for the Mac app.
- `license-worker.js` (35 KB, grep it): the licence Worker: merchant webhooks, keys, machine seats, cloud sync.
- `report-worker.js`: where the crash log's Send goes.
- `feedback-worker.js`: where Settings → Feedback goes: one D1 table (`feedback-schema.sql`) and a mail to support@ (PLAN 39a).
- `license-key.js`: `signLicenseKey`, `verifyLicenseKey` (Ed25519), `normalizePacks` (the payload's optional `packs` list, PLAN 45a-2).

## Contracts

- Imports only each other, `node:*` and `ws`; never `src/`.
- The browser side mirrors the wire: `lib/remoteProtocol.ts` (relay), `lib/licenseKey.ts` (keys), `lib/cloudSync.ts` (sync), `lib/crashLog.ts` (reports), `lib/feedback.ts` (feedback), `lib/fingerprint.ts` (song ID).
- `desktop/main.js` starts `remote-server.js` and imports `server-license.js` in its own process. The packed app carries only the server files named in `desktop/electron-builder.config.cjs` (`server` filter): a new file the show server imports goes on that list, or the app will not start (`signin-handoff.js` and `laptop.js` were missed once; the Mac app job caught it).
- Workers deploy with wrangler: `wrangler.toml` (song ID), `wrangler.report.toml` (reports), `wrangler.feedback.toml` (feedback, with its D1 database). Secrets live in the Worker's environment, never here.

## Files

- `abletonLink.js`: Ableton Link in, so the show takes the tempo and the bar from the music apps on the network. `startLink({ listInterfaces, rescanMs })` reads the interfaces again every 30 s. `remote-server.js` exports `linkOn()` and `onLinkChange(fn)` for the Mac app's Show menu; Link is Pro, beside OSC and Art-Net.
- `account-shows.js`: an account's running shows (served by the show server).
- `assistant.js`: the guard for the owner's AI assistant (PLAN 44b–44d): `readAssistantMode(env, file)` (the switch: `CHROMAGLASS_ASSISTANT`, or one word in `CHROMAGLASS_ASSISTANT_FILE`, default `server/.assistant`; off, suggest, play or full), `createAssistantGuard(send)`, `ROOM_KEYS` (from `assistant-room-keys.json`, the page's `RIG_KEYS` and `SETUP_KEYS`, rewritten by `node scripts/assistant.mjs --write`). `remote-server.js` admits the `assistant` role only from the laptop, with the show key, while the switch is on, and passes its every message through the guard. The guard also passes a `song-action` (PLAN 44f: Play for a pour, burst, dyes, zoom, kaleidoscope, look, title or a look setting; Full for the rest), and the relay answers `assistant-still` itself (44c-4, any mode on) and hands a `picture` marked `still` to the assistant alone. A `pour-text` (PLAN 37x-10c, words poured as ink) passes the guard in Play, rows and colour only; from a phone the relay passes it straight to the display, which reads it as untrusted (`src/lib/pourText.ts`). Since PLAN 44j a Full start of the stream may name saved destinations (refused with the saved list when one is not), and an `assistant-sound` (the sound source) passes in Full only and never while the display reports the stream on air; the relay drops either from a phone. Since PLAN 44k-4 to 44k-8 `FULL_ACTIONS`, `TEMPO_MIN_BPM` and `TEMPO_MAX_BPM` are exported; a `liquid` (sent as `bottle`) is refused when not on the bench the display reports, a `spin`'s finger comes in as `hand`, and `assistant-tempo` (Play), `assistant-link` (Full) and `assistant-seq` (Play, a sequence by name or a stage in range) pass the guard and are dropped from a phone. Since PLAN 44k-3 and 44k-5 `SET_FADES`, `SONG_NUDGE_MAX_S` and `ASSISTANT_ACTION_PREFIX` are exported; `assistant-set` (arm and cancel Suggest, Go now and the fade Play, the back plate Full; an item past the set refused with its length) and `assistant-songs` (read Suggest; remove, start, stop, nudge Play, a Play assistant taking out only its own `asst-` actions; Follow Full) pass the guard with `answer`, the number the display's `assistant-answer` comes back under (a song action carries it too, 44f-4), and are dropped from a phone; the relay sends an `assistant-answer` to the assistant only, and only from the display. Since PLAN 44k-2 an `assistant-music` (the music player) passes in Full, its rules in `assistantMusic.js`, and is dropped from a phone. Since PLAN 44k-10 an `assistant-ear` (calibrate, the song detector, Listen) passes in Full, its rules in `assistantEar.js`, and is dropped from a phone. Since PLAN 44k-11 an `assistant-record` (recording's options, a Take replayed, stopped or deleted) passes in Full, its rules in `assistantRecord.js`, and is dropped from a phone. `mcp/` imports `ROOM_KEYS`; nothing here imports `mcp/`.
- `artnet.js`: Art-Net in and out.
- `osc.js`: OSC both ways: the reader and the address space the show takes in (`readOsc`, `oscToMessage`), the writer and the feed it sends out (`encodeOsc`, `encodeBundle`, `feedMessages`, `oscOutTarget`; PLAN 13.5-osc).
- `cloud-sync.js`: Pro cloud sync store.
- `fingerprint-worker.js`: song ID proxy.
- `feedback-schema.sql`: the feedback table, run once against D1.
- `firebase-token.js`: `verifyFirebaseToken` (the shows and feedback Workers): `{ uid, aud, email, emailVerified }`, `emailVerified` added for feedback.
- `license-packs.js`: art packs sold on top of Home (`PACK_PRODUCTS`, `packForProduct`, `packFields`, `addPack`, `removePack`): a pack order signs the Home key again with the pack.
- `server-license.js`: local key storage for show server and Mac app; `currentStatus()` carries the key's `packs`. Also exports `fromAnotherSite(req)` and `saysJson(req)`, the loopback routes' guards.
- `assistantMusic.js`: the music player for the owner's assistant (PLAN 44k-2): `musicCall(message, player)` (the guard's rules after its mode and pace: a shelf track by title, a path admitted by `admitMusicFile`, a seek inside the track), `playerOf(state.player)`, `musicFolders(env)` (~/Music, or `CHROMAGLASS_MUSIC_DIR`), `admitMusicFile(path)` (a sound file, a real file, inside a music folder with links and `..` resolved; its one-off address and name), `serveMusicFile(req, res, url, laptop)` (`/assistant-music/<token>`, `MUSIC_PATH`, to the laptop alone, with Range), `MUSIC_TYPES`. Imported by `assistant.js` and `remote-server.js`; on the Mac app's `server` filter.
- `assistantEar.js`: calibrate, the song detector and Listen for the owner's assistant (PLAN 44k-10): `earCall(message, settings)` (the guard's rules after its mode and pace: calibrate refused with Auto Calibrate off, the detector a boolean, Name it a title and an artist). Imported by `assistant.js`; on the Mac app's `server` filter.
- `assistantRecord.js`: recording's options and the Takes for the owner's assistant (PLAN 44k-11): `recordCall(message, record)` (the guard's rules after its mode and pace: the Record panel's own options only, a container the display's encoder cannot write refused with the ones it can, a Take by its number sent on as its id, a number past the list refused with the count), `recordOf(state.record)`, `RECORD_MAKES`, `RECORD_QUALITIES`, `RECORD_FPS`, `RECORD_FORMATS`. Imported by `assistant.js`; on the Mac app's `server` filter.
- `laptop.js`: `isLaptop(req)` and `LOOPBACK`, the show server's rule for a request from the laptop itself (loopback socket, no forwarding headers, a loopback Host, which stops DNS rebinding); moved out of `remote-server.js` so `npm run signin` 25 imports the rule that ships.
- `signin-handoff.js`: `createSignInHandoff()`, `SIGNIN_HANDOFF_PATH` (`/signin-handoff`): the Mac app's Google or Apple sign-in carried from the browser to its window (PLAN 23.17f): a credential filed under the window's one-time code, in memory, taken once, two minutes at most, laptop and same-origin only. Mounted by `remote-server.js`.
- `license-key.js`, `license-worker.js`, `remote-server.js`, `report-worker.js`: above.

## Checks

Every file must pass `node --check` (Measure's "The scripts parse"). `npm run report-worker`, `feedback-worker`, `license-worker`, `fingerprint-worker`, `cloudsync`, `accountlink`, `signin` (25: the sign-in hand-off), `tiers`, `showkey`, `assistant` (the assistant role and guard), `lights` (Art-Net), `link` (Ableton Link), `oscout` (OSC out). The relay: `applink`, `remotemix`, `phonelink`, `draw`, `drawwall`, `tv`. The Mac app: `desktop`.
