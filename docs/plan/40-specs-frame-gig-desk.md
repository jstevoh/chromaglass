# 40. Specs for the thin items, part 1: the frame budget, the gig and the desk

**Written 2026-11-01** (the owner's split, adopted that day) for the ids the Order of work names that had no item text, check or number, so that Antigravity can take them. Part 2 (sound and beat, many plates, the fluids) is [`41-specs-sound-plates-fluids.md`](41-specs-sound-plates-fluids.md).

**How to read an entry.** `id (lane, size)`, then what is wrong or missing with the number that shows it, what to build and where, **Pass:** the number the change must reach, **Check:** the `npm run` script that prints it, and **Phone:** what the phone gets in the same PR. A figure marked *(read)* was counted from the code and is to be re-measured by the first run; a figure marked *(measured)* is a printed number from an earlier PR; a figure marked *(bar proposed)* is the bar this entry asks for, to be pinned by the person who writes the check against the first measured run, not by the builder. **A check marked *owed* does not exist yet or is not red on main: Claude writes it first, shown failing on main, and the builder never writes or loosens one** (`docs/handoff.md`). Where an id already has text elsewhere, the entry says where and adds only what was missing (a number, a check, a phone line).

The one-line index at the end of part 2 maps every id of the Order of work that is not a section number to the file that holds its text.

## The frame budget (Wave 4, lane C, M or S each)

Measure every item with `npm run stages` (the step's time by stage, alternate pairs, never one run each: its header says why) and `npm run encode` (dispatches, writes and bytes a step), at 512² on a thin gap, and quote the printed lines before and after in the commit. The home of H2c-1 to H2c-11 is `docs/webgpu-plan.md` "H2c", counted from the code on 2026-09-28; the list there is numbered 1 to 11 and these ids follow it: H2c-1 coarse levels in one workgroup, H2c-2 the warm start, H2c-3 `mgZero`, H2c-4 and H2c-11 shipped, H2c-5 first-order velocity, H2c-6 no aux target, H2c-7 the readback ring, H2c-8 air only where there are bubbles, H2c-9 pack after a step, H2c-10 grids a multiple of 32, H2c-labels the timestamp labels.

- **H2c-1 · The coarse levels in one workgroup** (C, M). Two V-cycles of two sweeps each way per level and sixteen at the coarsest (`MG_CYCLES`, `MG_SWEEPS`, `MG_COARSE_SWEEPS` in `src/gpu/fluid/constants.ts`), 442 projection dispatches a projection at 512² *(read)*, 172 to 216 of them on levels of 256 cells or fewer, 32 of those the sixteen coarsest sweeps on a 4² or 6² grid where a dispatch is nearly all overhead. Solve every level of 32² or less in one single-workgroup dispatch (b and p are 8 KB of workgroup memory), `workgroupBarrier` between sweeps, in a kernel beside the multigrid's in `src/gpu/wgsl/fluid/`, driven from `vcycle` in `src/gpu/fluid/passes.ts`. **Pass:** dispatches a step at least 35 % under what `npm run encode` prints on main the day you start (it printed 267 at 512² on a thin gap on 2026-10-24; the 2026-09-28 estimate was about 256 fewer of 485), *and* `pressureSelfTest`'s residual after the projection no worse than 1.05 times main's, both printed, *and* `stages`' projection median no higher. **Check:** `encode` and `stages` exist; the residual line and this item's dispatch line are written (2026-11-01, `scripts/encode-solve.mjs`): `npm run encode -- --owed=h2c1` prints main's 269 dispatches a step and the thin gap's residual 6.393e-2, the open plate's 2.071e-3, held at 1.05 times; take `h2c1` out of `--owed` in `scripts/checks.json` when it passes. **Phone:** none (solver only).
- **H2c-3 · `mgZero` folded into the first sweep** (C, S). The multigrid zeroes each level's pressure with its own dispatch (`mgZero`, `fluid/core.ts:710`, `passes.ts:425`) before the first sweep reads it as a guess of zero. Make the first sweep of a level read zero instead of a buffer (a `first` flag in the kernel's argument), and drop the dispatch. **Pass:** 28 fewer dispatches a step *(read)*, the residual unchanged to the printed digit. **Check:** `encode`: the line "no mgZero dispatch" (44 a step on main) and the residual lines, written 2026-11-01; take `h2c3` out of `--owed`. Do it before H2c-1 if both are open: H2c-1 removes the levels this touches.
- **H2c-2 · A warm start and one V-cycle** (C, M, after H2c-1 and H2c-3). Start each projection's pressure from the last step's (the plate changes little in a sixtieth of a second) and run one V-cycle, not two (`MG_CYCLES`): about 218 fewer dispatches *(read)*. The warm start is `docs/evaluation-2026-09.md`'s. It is a physics change in the sense that the projection must stay as incompressible as before: **Pass:** `pressureSelfTest`'s residual no worse than 1.5 times main's after one cycle, *and* `npm run physics` and `npm run finite` unchanged, *and* the dispatches a step down by at least 150 against `encode` on main. Judge by the residual, not by eye. **Check:** `encode`, `physics`, `finite` exist; written 2026-11-01: one V-cycle a projection (2 on main, thin and open) and at most 119 dispatches a step, the residual bar 1.5 times main's once `h2c2` is out of `--owed` (the line switches by itself; nobody moves it). This is a Claude brief first (the residual bar is Claude's to pin, per the split).
- **H2c-5 · First-order velocity** (C, S). The CPU solver's own comment says MacCormack "costs a third of the step on a field nobody sees directly", and transports velocity first order; the GPU still runs MacCormack on it (`advect velocity`, two full-grid passes). Move velocity to first-order semi-Lagrangian and keep MacCormack on the dye. **Pass:** two fewer full-grid passes a step; `stages` advect-velocity row down by about its share; the dye's sharpness unchanged (`npm run ab` on one plate, the "hard edges" percentage within 1 point of main's, both printed) because the dye is what is seen. **Check:** `stages`, `ab` exist. **Phone:** none. Physics at the core: say in the PR that first-order velocity diffuses momentum numerically and name the viscosity it adds at 512², measured with `npm run physics`.
- **H2c-6 · No aux target while the camera is off** (C, S). The display writes its second attachment every frame (`src/gpu/plate.ts`): 4 bytes a pixel, about 16 MB a frame at 2560×1600 *(read)*. Build a one-target variant of the display pipeline used when the camera pass is off, keyed in the pipeline cache by that flag. **Pass:** the aux texture not allocated with the camera off (printed from the stage's resource count), the frame's pixels identical to main's with the camera off (`npm run pixels` unchanged) and `stages`' display row down. **Check:** `pixels`, `stages`; a texture-count line *owed*. **Phone:** none, but the phone's `npm run phone` stays green.
- **H2c-7 · Readback straight into its ring** (C, S). Each field goes texture → `rbStaging` → ring slot, 1.18 MB of copying a layer a frame *(read)*, and the downsample and staging copy run when both slots are busy, which is when the GPU is behind (`src/gpu/fluid/reads.ts`, `readGate.ts`). Look for a free ring slot first and copy straight into it; skip the downsample when none is free. **Pass:** bytes copied a frame down by half at least, printed by `npm run framereads` (it counts the reads), and a ring with both slots busy asks the GPU for nothing (0 downsample dispatches in that phase). **Check:** `framereads` exists; the busy-slots phase is *owed* if it does not print it. Follows 14ab-3 (shipped, #549). **Phone:** none.
- **H2c-8 · Air only where there are bubbles** (C, S). Every layer makes an rgba16float air pair (4 MiB at 512²) and clears it with a render pass every step, background layers that never get a bubble included; and since the bubble list is set once a frame while `which` flips every step, a frame of several steps lands its whole rate in the first. Allocate and clear the air pair only for a layer that has a bubble (allocate on the first bubble, free after a quiet minute), and spread a frame's bubble rate over its steps. **Pass:** a plate with no bubbles clears 0 air targets a step (a count printed by `stages` or `encode`), 4 MiB less allocated a bubble-less layer at 512², and `npm run bubblephysics` and `npm run pops` unchanged to the printed digit. **Check:** `bubblephysics`, `pops`, `stages`; the target count is *owed*. **Phone:** none.
- **H2c-9 · Pack only after a step** (C, S, with 14j). `packDye` and `packVel` also run on frames the solver did not step, half of all frames at 120 Hz. Skip both when no step ran since the last pack and nothing was drawn into the fields. **Pass:** at a display running 120 Hz against 60 steps, pack dispatches a second at most 61 (printed), plate unchanged (`pixels`). **Check:** `framereads`' counting harness is the model; the pack count is *owed*. **Phone:** none. Rides with 14j's draw cap (14-show-at-the-gig.md §14j); this is the part that stays after the cap.
- **H2c-10 · Pinned grids a multiple of 32** (C, S). `resolveSimResolution` rounds to even only, so `?sim=514` or `?sim=1000` (or a saved look) gives a coarsest multigrid level of N/2 or N/8 and a V-cycle little better than sweeps. Round a pinned grid to the nearest multiple of 32 (up on a tie) and say so in the readout. **Pass:** for N in 514, 1000, 777, 100, `resolveSimResolution(N) % 32 === 0` and the projection residual after a step within 2× of the 512² plate's (printed); a saved look with a pinned grid loads and plays (`npm run saves`). **Check:** the rounding line is written in `npm run rungs` (2026-11-01, id `grid32`; main: 514, 1000, 776, 100); the residual-within-2× clause is still to write (`encode-solve.mjs` reads a residual at any grid). **Phone:** none.
- **H2c-labels · Timestamp labels kept with their ring slot** (C, S, with 14r). The labels are the frame just issued while the data is a copy one to three frames old (`src/gpu/kit.ts`), so when the list of passes changes (particles, the camera, the finish, the output) the times land on the wrong labels for a moment and `gpuFrameMs` is skewed. Store the label list with its ring slot and read the times against the list of the frame that wrote them. The flash probe's pixel count has the same fault (14r, in the quarter-wall brief). **Pass:** with a pass added mid-run (toggle the camera on at frame 100), no frame in the next 4 reports a pass label that did not exist in the frame it timed (a node line over the kit's slots), and `gpuFrameMs` on that frame is within 10 % of the frame before. **Check:** a node harness over `kit.ts`'s ring is *owed* (Claude). **Phone:** none.

## Governor, wall and projector (Wave 4 and lane A, S each)

- **14b-120 · A 120 Hz desk with a 60 Hz projector draws 120 a second** (A, S). - **14c-governor · A wall coming or going keeps the governor's memory** (A, S).
