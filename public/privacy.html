Privacy

ChromaGlass · last changed 1 November 2026

ChromaGlass is a liquid light show that runs in your browser, in the Mac app and in the phone apps. Almost all of it happens on your own machine: the liquid, the sound it listens to and the looks you save never leave it. This page lists everything that does leave it, where it goes and why. ChromaGlass is made by Plum Tree Art LLC, a California limited liability company ("we").

We do not sell anything about you, show you ads, or use analytics or tracking of any kind.

Your account

To use the site you sign in with Google, with a link we email you, or with an email and a password. Sign-in is run by Google's Firebase Authentication, which keeps:

  • your email address,
  • if you choose a password, the password in scrambled (hashed) form: it is held by Firebase, never by our servers, and we cannot read it,
  • an account id it makes for you,
  • which way you signed in, and when you made the account and last signed in.

While a signed-in show is running with its local show server, your account also holds a short-lived entry for that running show (its name, its local network address and its show key, expiring after 90 seconds once the show closes) so a phone, tablet or laptop signed in to the same account can join it with a tap. Once linked, the remote talks to the show directly over your own network. To check that a phone asking for those shows is really signed in to your account, the show server fetches Google's public sign-in keys from www.googleapis.com; nothing about you is sent with it. If you buy a Home or Pro licence, our Cloudflare Worker also keeps your signed licence key and up to two activated machine identifiers under your email address in Workers KV, so signing in restores your licence and lets you move it between machines. If you have Pro and leave Cloud sync on in Profile & Store, the same Worker keeps a copy of your saved looks, sequences, set lists, song shows, custom liquids, MIDI map and app settings under your account, so every computer you sign in to has them; Erase the cloud copy on that page deletes it, and deleting your account deletes it too.

Firebase sends the emails an email-and-password account needs (confirm your address, choose a new password). One email address is one account: signing in with Google and with a password on the same address reach the same account and the same licence.

We use your email to sign you in and, if you buy a licence, to send you your key and receipt. We only send product updates or newsletter emails if you explicitly tick the optional opt-in box on the sign-in screen or in Profile & Store (stored as an opt-in flag and timestamp under your email in Workers KV), and you can unsubscribe at any time in /profile or at /unsubscribe. We never hand or sell your address to anyone.

Deleting your account

The account sheet (on the desk and on the phone) has Delete account. It deletes your account and everything above from Firebase straight away. Looks and settings saved in your browser stay there until you clear them; they were never ours.

You can also ask us to delete it, or ask what we hold about you, at support@chromaglass.art.

What stays on your machine

  • Microphone and camera. When you let ChromaGlass hear a microphone or see a camera, the sound and picture are read in your browser to move the liquid. They are not recorded or sent anywhere, except for song identification below.
  • Your looks, presets and settings are kept in your browser's storage.
  • Recordings you make with Record are written to your own disk.
  • The phone link. A phone or tablet you link to the show talks to it directly over your own network. Nothing passes through us.
  • Stage lights. If you run the show's own small server and point it at a lighting desk (Art-Net), it sends the plate's colours to that desk over your own network. It is off unless you set it up.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Song identification (when it is switched on for the site, and only while ChromaGlass is listening to sound): a four-second sound clip is sent through our own small server at Cloudflare to AudD or ACRCloud, which answer with the song's name. We do not keep the clip. Their own privacy terms cover what they do with it.
  • Lyrics: once a song is known, its artist, title, album and length are sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free lyrics library, to fetch its words.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which sees your address and browser like any website does.
  • Crash reports are never sent on their own. If something goes wrong and you press Send on the report sheet, we receive what the sheet shows you first: your note, the page address, your browser, screen and graphics chip, the app's own log, and, unless you untick them, the look and its settings and a small picture of the plate. Each report is stored at Cloudflare with the time and the country it came from, not your address, and is deleted after 90 days. To stop anyone flooding us with reports, your IP address is counted for one hour and then dropped.
  • Feedback is sent only when you press Send in Settings → Feedback, and the pane shows you what goes before you do. We receive your note, the topic you picked, the app's version, your browser, screen and graphics chip, the look's name and your plan, and, unless you turn them off, the app's recent log with the look's settings and a small picture of the plate; passwords, licence keys, stream keys and sign-in tokens are taken out of the log first. If you are signed in we keep your account's id and its verified email address with the note, so we can answer you; if not, only the address you choose to type. Each note is stored at Cloudflare with the time and the country it came from, and kept so we can follow what people ask for over time; write to support@chromaglass.art and we will delete yours. A copy is emailed to support@chromaglass.art through Resend, the service that sends it. To stop anyone flooding us, a one-way hash of your IP address is counted for one hour and then dropped.
  • Paid licences: when you buy or upgrade a Home or Pro licence from the account sheet, checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy (chromaglass.lemonsqueezy.com), our merchant of record, which processes your payment and sends our Cloudflare Worker your email address and order details so your signed licence key can be issued and attached to your account. To check that a request for your keys really comes from your sign-in, that Worker fetches Google's public sign-in keys from www.googleapis.com; nothing about you is sent with it.
  • The site itself is served by Google's Firebase Hosting, and our small servers run on Cloudflare. Like every web host they see your IP address and keep short-lived logs to keep the service running.

The phone and tablet apps

The ChromaGlass apps for iPhone, iPad and Android are free, sell nothing inside them and collect nothing the website does not. Everything on this page applies to them as it does to the site.

  • iPhone and iPad. The app carries the show inside it, so playing needs no connection. It asks the phone's own permission before it hears the microphone, sees the camera, reads the motion sensors for Tilt or adds a recording to your Photos; each is used on the phone, for the reason it asks, and nothing it hears, sees or saves is sent anywhere. Used as the remote for a laptop's show, it talks to that laptop over your own network.
  • Android. The app is this website opened in Chrome without the address bar, so it is the website in every way this page describes, and Chrome's own permission prompts cover the microphone and camera.

Neither app tracks you across other companies' apps or websites, shows ads or uses an advertising identifier.

Children

ChromaGlass is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep an account for one. If you think we have, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

When what we keep changes, this page changes first, with its date at the top. The full history of this page is public in the project's source code.
