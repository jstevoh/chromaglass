ChromaGlass, for writing about it.

ChromaGlass is a liquid light show you play to your music: oil, ink and ferrofluid on a glass plate, simulated drop by drop on the computer's graphics chip and thrown onto a wall or a projector. It listens to the room or follows MIDI clock and Ableton Link, so the colour moves with the song at a gig or in a living room, free in Chrome, with a Mac app for shows.

Free to quote as is.

The facts

WhatA real-time fluid simulation of a 1960s liquid light show (the overhead projector, the clock glass and the oil and water), played like an instrument with fingers, a mouse, MIDI controllers and sound.
PriceFree in the browser, with every look, tool and liquid. Home $35 adds full-length recording, 4K renders, the phone remote and the Mac app. Pro $199 adds OSC, Art-Net / DMX, the finest grid and your own liquids. Pay once, no subscription.
Runs onCurrent Chrome or Edge with WebGPU, and the Mac app on Apple silicon Macs (M1 and later). A phone or tablet plays it too, or drives a laptop's show as a remote.
