Sync and control

The plate follows your clock and your hands. Anything that speaks MIDI or OSC can drive it, and every control in the app can be learned.

MIDI clock and timecode All tiers

  • MIDI clock in: the beat clock follows the tempo; Start, Continue and Stop start and stop the show.
  • MIDI Time Code at 24, 25, 29.97 and 30 fps, full-frame and quarter-frame, for a show locked to a timeline.
  • No clock? A phase-locked beat tracker hears the kick, or tap the tempo, or type the BPM.

MIDI controllers All tiers

  • MIDI learn for any knob, fader or pad, CC or note, with four banks, soft takeover and endless encoders.
  • Ready-made maps for the APC mini mk2, APC40 mkII, Launch Control XL, Launchpad Mini mk3 / Launchpad X and nanoKONTROL2, with the pads lit to match.
  • Anything else (a MIDI Fighter Twister, a Faderfox, a sequencer's CC lane) works through learn.

OSC in Pro · Mac app

The Mac app listens on UDP 9000 once you turn on Show → OSC input, for this Mac only or for the whole network. Send it from Resolume, TouchDesigner, Max, Ableton's Connection Kit, TouchOSC, VCV Rack or a norns script.

/chromaglass/setting/<key> 0.8
/chromaglass/action/<name>      seq-next, blackout-toggle, …
/chromaglass/preset <id>
/chromaglass/drop  x y [amount]
/chromaglass/blow  x y [amount] [dx dy]
/chromaglass/press x y [amount]
/chromaglass/tilt  x y
/chromaglass/dye   #rrggbb

From Resolume (Arena or Avenue 7). Its OSC output speaks its own addresses, like /composition/layers/1/video/opacity, so each control you want to send needs a custom address of ours:

  1. In ChromaGlass, turn on Show → OSC input → This Mac only.
  2. In Resolume, open Preferences → OSC, turn on OSC Output, send to Localhost (127.0.0.1) and set the outgoing port to 9000.
  3. Choose Shortcuts → Edit OSC (⇧⌘O) and click the slider you want to send, such as a layer's opacity.
  4. Right-click it and enable OSC output (or enable it in the Shortcuts panel), pick that output in the panel's dropdown, and replace the address Resolume filled in with the Custom Address /chromaglass/setting/audioImpact.
  5. Press Esc, then move the slider: Sound Drive follows it.

Send a number from 0 to 1. Sound Drive, Dimmer, Bloom, Turbulence and Plate Rock are 0 to 1 already. A setting with another range (Speed runs 0 to 0.3) takes the number as it is and stops at its own top, so put those on MIDI learn. Steps are from Resolume's OSC manual.

Your phone as the remote Home · Pro

The Mac app runs its own show server. Any phone or iPad on the venue's Wi-Fi opens the remote in its browser with the show's key: looks, dyes and the mixer, with nothing to install and nothing going through the cloud.

On the way: MIDI notes as voices (each note pours its own dye) and OSC out.

With Ableton Live and other DAWs

There is no plugin yet, so here is the honest route today. It uses nothing but standard MIDI and audio, which every DAW speaks.

Tempo and transport

Turn on MIDI clock (and MTC if you like) to the Mac's IAC Driver bus in your DAW. ChromaGlass follows the tempo, and Play and Stop in the DAW start and stop the show.

Automation

Draw CC automation on a MIDI track sent to the same bus, then MIDI-learn each lane to a control: the look changes, swells and blackouts happen on the bar you wrote them. In the Mac app, Connection Kit's OSC sends work too.

Audio

The Mac app hears system audio straight from macOS, so it hears whatever Live is playing with no loopback driver. In the browser, route the master into an input (an interface's line in, or a loopback device) and pick it.

On the way: an AU / VST3 bridge plugin that sends the host's transport, automatable slots and the track's audio to the app, and Ableton Link. Both are in the public plan with no date yet.

Sound in

The plate is driven by the sound itself: it answers each kick and note in the liquid, and never by shaking the whole frame.

System audio Home · Pro · Mac app

Whatever the Mac is playing, captured through macOS's own ScreenCaptureKit: Live, Logic, a streaming service, a DJ app. No tab to share, no picker, no loopback driver.

Inputs All tiers

Any input the browser lists: an interface's line in, a mixer's send, a microphone in the room, or a shared browser tab's audio. A line or mic input is read as one mono channel today, with the browser's echo, noise and level processing turned off.

On the way: patch inputs, so every channel of an interface can be a voice, a gate, CV or a clock (1, 2, 4, 24 or 48 PPQN) with reset. A browser gets at most two channels from an interface, so all of them will come through the Mac app.

Song ID and lyrics

Timed lyrics (from LRCLIB) for every tier. In the Mac app, Home and Pro also identify the song playing in the room and step through that song's look script.

The show runs offline Home · Pro · Mac app

The app carries the whole show and its server inside it, and its licence checks offline, so a venue with no internet is fine. Song ID and lyric lookups are the only things that need a connection.

Projectors and walls

The plate is drawn at the projector's own resolution, not stretched from a window.

One cable, no clicks

On HDMI or USB-C, the site sends the show to the projector in one click. The Mac app does it with no click: plug the projector in and the show is on it, full screen, and back on it after a re-plug. The display does not sleep, and the show keeps time when its window is covered.

Mapping All tiers

  • Rear-projection flip, four-corner keystone, feathered edge masks, gain and gamma.
  • Up to 16 mapped surfaces on the output, each showing the wall, the front plate, the back plate or a film, with soft edges where they overlap, lined up by eye.

Safe for a room

A built-in limiter reads what actually reached the screen and holds sustained flashing under three a second, the photosensitive-seizure guideline, while a single hard hit on a kick is left alone. It is on by default. If the GPU is lost (a projector plugged in mid-set, a driver reset), the show recovers by itself without losing the cue list.

Into someone else's rig

Today, capture the projector window in OBS and send it on with OBS's NDI or Syphon plugin into Resolume, MadMapper, HeavyM or TouchDesigner.

On the way: Syphon and NDI straight out of the Mac app, and several projector outputs from one Mac, each with its own corner pin and a blended span between them.

Lights

Art-Net / DMX out Pro · Mac app

The room's lights take the plate's colours: any universe, up to 170 fixtures, RGB, GRB, BRG, RGBW and dimmer orders, at up to 44 frames a second. When the glass thins the room dims, and a blackout on the desk is a blackout on the rig.

Art-Net in Pro · Mac app

A lighting desk drives the show: map any channel of a universe onto any setting, so the desk holds the running order.

Today both are set with environment variables when the show server starts (the guide has the list). On the way: a panel in the app for them.

Recording and rendering

Record the show

The output as you play it, with its sound: WebM (VP9 and Opus) or MP4 (H.264), at the canvas's size and 60 fps, at 5, 12 or 30 Mbps. Free clips stop at 15 seconds with a small corner mark; Home and Pro record the whole set with no mark.

Render a song

An offline bounce, frame by frame, so it never drops a frame however heavy the look: H.264 or VP9 with the track's audio, at 720p, 1080p or 4K, square or vertical 1080, at 24, 30 or 60 fps. Free renders stop at 720p with the mark.

Takes

Record your hands rather than the picture: a take keeps every drop, blow and control move, and plays them back on the live plate.

Your name on it Home · Pro

Your own logo or SVG on the glass instead of the ChromaGlass mark. What you record and render is yours to post, sell and screen.

On the way: going live to YouTube straight from the Mac app.

Questions

Does it work with Ableton Live?

Through MIDI and audio, yes, with no plugin: send MIDI clock and CC automation to the IAC bus, MIDI-learn the lanes, and let the Mac app hear Live through system audio. We have not yet published a Live set tested end to end with it; that, Ableton Link and an AU / VST3 bridge plugin are on the way. The route in full.

Can I feed it into Resolume, MadMapper or TouchDesigner?

Not directly yet: there is no Syphon or NDI output today. Capture the projector window in OBS and send it on with OBS's NDI or Syphon plugin. Going the other way, Resolume, TouchDesigner and Max can drive ChromaGlass over OSC (Pro).

How many projectors can it drive?

One output from the Mac, at the projector's native resolution, which you can map into up to 16 surfaces with keystone, masks and soft overlapping edges. Several physical outputs from one Mac, each with its own corner pin, are planned for Pro.

Can I sync it to my modular or a hardware sequencer?

Yes, by MIDI clock: anything that sends clock and Start / Stop over USB or a MIDI interface. VCV Rack (with an OSC module) and norns can send OSC to the Mac app. Any audio you patch into an interface input drives the plate. Gate, CV and clock inputs on every channel of an interface are planned.

Do I need the internet at the gig?

Not with the Mac app. It carries the show inside it and checks its licence offline. Only song ID and lyric lookups need a connection.

Can I sell what I record?

Yes. What you record and render is yours to post, sell and screen (the free tier's clips carry a small corner mark). The Terms have the details.

Will my laptop run it?

The Mac app needs an Apple silicon Mac. The app measures what each frame costs on your GPU and picks the grid to hold the frame rate, so a lighter Mac runs a coarser grid rather than dropping frames. Try your looks free in Chrome on the same Mac first.

Is it a subscription? How many computers?

No subscription: Home and Pro are paid once and include at least a year of updates. A key works on two Macs at a time, and you can move it from the profile sheet. Home to Pro is $164.

Windows?

Not yet. Nothing has been tested on a Windows PC, so we don't sell it there. A Windows app is in the plan.
