# ChromaGlass press media

Stills and clips for the press page (PLAN 37k), taken from the minute-long site
films of 2026-10-29 (PLAN 34a-6). Every frame is the app's own Song film: the
liquid is simulated as it moves and answers its music, at 2560 × 1440 and 60 fps,
seed 1, with nothing drawn or edited in afterwards. Free to use in coverage of
ChromaGlass; please credit "ChromaGlass" and, for the clips, the music below.

## Clips

1920 × 1080, 60 fps, H.264 (CRF 22, capped at 4.3 Mb/s to stay under 12 MB),
yuv420p, AAC 160 kb/s stereo with half-second fades, `+faststart`. Each is the
20 s of its film with the most frame-to-frame movement, measured as the mean
luma change between frames at 10 fps.
